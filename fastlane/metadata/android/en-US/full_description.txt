Selfband lets you record multitrack videos by yourself. Record a base track, then record the other parts while listening to the previous ones on headphones, and put everything together in a video collage with mixing and editing in real time. It is a one-person band on your phone.

ACCURATE SYNC
• Each track is recorded while the others play back, and it lands in the project already aligned.
• Latency is measured on your own phone and audio gear (a quick calibration with headphones or a loopback cable), so it fits any device.
• The audio clock leads and the video follows it, so a clap lines up with its picture.

WORKS WITH YOUR GEAR
• The phone microphone, wired headphones, any USB class-compliant audio interface, or Bluetooth (with a warning about its latency).
• Nothing is tailored to a brand or model: the app uses what it detects, what it measures and what you choose.

RECORDING
• Front or back camera, with resolution, frame rate, stabilization, a framing grid and a "ghost" of another track to match your position.
• Metronome with count-in, takes per track, and audio-only tracks.
• No artificial limits on the number of tracks or the length: only your phone's hardware and storage.

CANVAS AND LAYOUT
• Vertical (9:16) for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts, horizontal (16:9) for YouTube, and square (1:1).
• Ready-made layouts, a free layout editor (position, size, crop, guides and snapping), canvas background, and your own saved templates.

MIXER
• Volume, pan, mute and solo per track, level meters, master volume and a limiter.
• Two effects only, on purpose: EQ per track and a reverb send.
• Fine offset by dragging the waveform, and global trim.

EXPORT
• MP4 video in H.264 or HEVC, 720p to 4K, 30 or 60 fps.
• WAV of the mix and of each dry track (stems).
• Saved to your gallery and ready to share. Projects with many tracks use lightweight preview copies so playback stays smooth.

PRIVATE AND FREE
• No internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no account. Everything stays on your phone.
• Free software under the GNU GPL v3 or later. Source code: https://github.com/higor0227/selfband

Available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
